About Me

I am an experimental physicist specialising in atomic and molecular beams, gas–surface interactions, laser spectroscopy, and precision instrumentation. My work combines fundamental research with the design and development of advanced experimental systems. My research focuses on stereodynamically controlled reactive scattering on metal surfaces, alongside quantum state-resolved scattering and high-resolution laser diagnostics. I have developed strong expertise in building experimental tools from the ground up, including time-of-flight mass spectrometers, ion and velocity map imaging systems, photoacoustic instruments, compact beam sources, and a high-precision interferometric wavemeter.

A key strength of my work is developing custom, high-performance instrumentation that is not commercially available. I integrate optics, vacuum systems, electronics, and data acquisition to create robust and precise measurement platforms. I am particularly interested in solving complex experimental challenges through in-house design, validation, and optimisation.

Current Focus

  • Development of compact high-flux, low-divergence atomic and molecular beam sources.
  • Helium scattering, molecular beam diagnostics, and beam manipulation using magnetic fields.
  • Precision laser diagnostics including interferometric wavemeters and frequency-stabilisation tools.
  • Custom experimental systems for gas--surface dynamics, spectroscopy, and ultra-high-vacuum studies.
