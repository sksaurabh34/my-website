About Me

I am an experimental physicist working at the interface of atomic and molecular beam science, gas--surface dynamics, laser spectroscopy, and precision instrumentation. My research combines fundamental studies of molecule--surface interactions with the development of advanced experimental tools, including compact beam sources, interferometric laser diagnostics, ion-optical detection systems, and ultra-high-vacuum instrumentation.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Assistant at Swansea University, where I work on helium scattering, beam-source development, and precision diagnostics for surface and beam experiments. My broader interests include beam manipulation, state control, laser-frequency diagnostics, and the design of compact high-performance experimental systems.
